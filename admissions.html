Admissions 2027-28

Admission Notice

Admissions are open for the Academic Session 2027-28 for students from Nursery to Class IX. Parents seeking admission for their children are requested to visit the school office during working hours for registration and further guidance.

Admission is subject to seat availability and fulfilment of the school's admission requirements.

Orientation & Joining Instructions (Classes I to X)

  • Students must attend school regularly and maintain punctuality.
  • School uniform must be worn neatly on all working days.
  • Students should carry prescribed books, notebooks and stationery daily.
  • Homework and assignments should be completed on time.
  • Respect for teachers, fellow students and school property is expected at all times.
  • Participation in academic and co-curricular activities is encouraged.

Orientation & Joining Instructions (Pre-Primary)

  • Children should be accompanied by a parent or guardian on the first day.
  • Parents are encouraged to help children develop confidence and independence.
  • All personal belongings should be clearly labelled.
  • Regular attendance helps children settle comfortably into the school environment.
  • Parents should maintain communication with teachers regarding their child's progress.

Books & Uniform

  • The prescribed book list for each class will be provided after admission confirmation.
  • Students should use only the books recommended by the school.
  • School uniforms must follow the approved pattern and specifications.
  • Students are expected to maintain proper dress code and personal hygiene.
  • Further details regarding books and uniforms will be shared during the admission process.

Documents Required

  • Birth Certificate
  • Passport Size Photographs
  • Aadhaar Card of Student
  • Aadhaar Card of Parent / Guardian
  • Previous School Report Card (if applicable)
  • Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
  • Proof of Residence

School Timings

Monday to Saturday
School Opening Time: 8:00 AM
School Closing Time: 3:00 PM

Fee Information

Detailed fee information can be obtained directly from the school office. Parents are requested to contact the administration for the latest fee structure and admission-related charges.
